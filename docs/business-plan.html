3 Our Solution

PlainLedger is a Context Intelligence Platform that connects to your accounting software, learns from your decisions, and delivers recommendations grounded in what actually worked for businesses like yours.

How It Works

1. Connect (2 minutes)

One-click integration with QuickBooks, Xero, and Sage. We securely sync your financial data to our Canadian-hosted infrastructure.

2. Analyze (Real-time)

Our AI analyzes your data against 75,000+ Canadian businesses in your industry. We calculate industry-specific KPIs, detect anomalies, and identify opportunities.

3. Recommend (Compounding)

Get recommendations backed by actual outcomes from similar businesses. "Restaurants your size who faced this margin gap increased prices by 8% with 94% success rate."

4. Capture & Learn (The Flywheel)

When you make a decision, we capture the context. When you see results, we track the outcome. This creates a feedback loop that makes recommendations more accurate over time.

Why Multi-Provider?

  • Automatic Fallback: If one provider is down, we seamlessly switch
  • Best-in-Class Selection: Route to the optimal model for each task type
  • Cost Optimization: Use lite models for simple tasks, full tier for complex analysis
  • Zero Downtime: Circuit breaker pattern ensures 99.9% availability

Proprietary Training Pipeline

Component Technology Purpose
Base Model Mistral 7B Best performance/cost ratio for instruction tuning
Fine-Tuning LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation) Efficient training without full model weights
Knowledge Base AWS Bedrock + OpenSearch RAG for industry pattern retrieval
Inference AWS SageMaker Serverless Cost-efficient, scalable deployment
Data Pipeline AWS Glue + S3 Data Lake ETL for training data preparation

Open Source Foundation

Built on proven open-source technologies: Mistral 7B, HuggingFace Transformers, PyTorch, LoRA. This ensures auditability, reduces vendor lock-in, and enables cost-efficient fine-tuning.
